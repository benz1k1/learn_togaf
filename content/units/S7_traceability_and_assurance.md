title: "Traceability and Assurance"
---
Learning unit **S7 — Traceability and Assurance**: two-way traceability (top-down justification of every control, bottom-up completeness for every attribute), the assurance framework that produces the evidence that traceability holds, and the Matrix used as a viewpoint catalogue and a completeness check. This is the F2 module's closing theme and the part of SABSA that TOGAF's compliance reviews most resemble.
