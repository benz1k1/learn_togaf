title: "Technology and Physical Layers"
---
Learning unit **A6 — Technology and Physical Layers**: the green elements of the ArchiMate core,
the artifact and its deployment and realization relationships to the application layer, and the
physical extension for equipment, facilities, distribution networks and material. Each lesson
ends with a check-yourself drill drawn from the OGA-031 bank and the elements it names are
flashcards.
