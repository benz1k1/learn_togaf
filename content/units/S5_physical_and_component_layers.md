---
title: "Physical and Component Layers"
---
Learning unit **S5 — Physical and Component Layers**: the builder's and the tradesman's views of the SABSA Matrix, where logical security services become mechanisms, data structures and platforms, and then products, tools and standards. The unit ends with the three-step drop from service to mechanism to component, which is SABSA's counterpart to TOGAF's ABB → SBB → product.
