Learning unit **S3 — Business Attributes and the Risk Model**: the Business Attributes Profile with its taxonomy of families, the metric type, measurement approach and performance target that make an attribute a requirement, and SABSA's two-sided risk model with its five control types, operational risk framework and attribute-centred risk assessment. This is the content that the Motivation column of the Matrix carries from top to bottom.
