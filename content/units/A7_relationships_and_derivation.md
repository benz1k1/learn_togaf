---
title: "Relationships and Derivation"
---
Learning unit **A7 — Relationships and Derivation**: the eleven ArchiMate relationships and the junction, the derivation rules that let a summary model stay true, and the Appendix B table that says which relationship may join any two element types. It is the most heavily weighted ArchiMate unit and the one Part 1 candidates most often fail, so every lesson ends with a check-yourself drill and the direction of every arrow is treated as examinable.
