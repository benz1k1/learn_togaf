title: "Lifecycle and Service Management"
---
Learning unit **S6 — Lifecycle and Service Management**: the SABSA Lifecycle (Strategy and Planning, Design, Implement, Manage and Measure) and the Service Management layer that runs across every Matrix column, from operations and incident handling to change, assurance and audit. The last lesson lines the lifecycle up against the ADM and TOGAF architecture governance so the two loops can be studied as one.
