---
title: "Logical Layer, Domains and Trust"
---
Learning unit **S4 — Logical Layer, Domains and Trust**: the designer's view of the SABSA Matrix with its security services and information classification, the domain model of security domains, sub-domains, multi-tiered domains and policy authorities, and the trust relationships — one-way, two-way, transitive, brokered — with the inter-domain services that carry interactions across policy boundaries. This is the Design phase of the Lifecycle and the SABSA side of TOGAF's Phases B and C.
